Kairos is an offline music player for the songs already on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and no internet permission.

<b>Synced lyrics</b>
• Karaoke-style lyrics that fill line by line as the song plays
• Reads lyrics embedded in MP3, FLAC and M4A files, and .lrc files next to your songs
• Tap a line to jump there; fix the timing per song

<b>Sound</b>
• Hi-res 32-bit output, and bit-perfect USB output on Android 14+
• 10-band equalizer, bass boost and virtualizer
• ReplayGain and gapless playback
• Sleep timer that fades out or stops after the current song

<b>Your library</b>
• Songs, albums, artists and genres with a fast A–Z scroller
• Songs with several artists show under each artist
• Playlists, favourites and auto playlists: Most played, On repeat, Recently added, Forgotten gems and genre mixes
• Listening stats: time listened, top songs and artists, streaks
• Choose which folders to scan

<b>Design</b>
• The app takes its colour from the album cover that is playing
• Swipe the mini player up into the full player, and swipe the cover to change song
• Light, dark and pure-black themes

Plays MP3, FLAC, M4A/AAC, OGG, Opus and WAV. Android 9 or newer.
